# RR5f decision

Decision: **RR5f-RECOMMEND-A**

Adopt supplied ranks as the operative specification for the main estimator, inference theory, Monte Carlo, and empirical application. Remove the cap+1 ridge-ratio selector, its pilot-only identification extension, and its consistency theorem from the submitted paper. Preserve all historical theory, code, and RR5/RR5e evidence in the repository.

This decision does not interpret RR5/RR5e as statistical selector failure. The selector was never evaluated because no pilot was accepted. It recognizes that the current pilot is computationally unusable under the maintained numerical definition and declines to relax that definition post hoc.

The next phase is a manuscript-only redesign following `rr5f_recommendation.md`; it requires separate authorization. RR5f performs no implementation, manuscript edit, fitting, inference, tuning, or Monte Carlo.
