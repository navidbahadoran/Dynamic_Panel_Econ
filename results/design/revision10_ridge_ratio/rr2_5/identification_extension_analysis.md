# Identification extension analysis

## What Revision 9 formally assumes

Part (i) of `a:identification` requires

`E_0[sum_it Y_it(Delta)^2 | C] >= c ||Delta||_F^2`

only for nonzero differences `Delta=Theta-Theta_0` generated by candidates with ranks in `R_max` and the
coefficient box. It does not quantify over `D_max^+`. Therefore cap+1 identification is not a logical
consequence of the displayed Revision-9 assumption.

## Rank-independent primitive sufficient condition

Stack the regressors multiplying the block entries at `(i,t)` as

`z_it=(y_i,t-1,...,y_i,t-P,x_it,1)`

and the corresponding coefficient differences as `delta_it`. Then

`Y_it(Delta)=z_it' delta_it`.

If the primitive conditional joint-regressor Gram satisfies

`lambda_min(E_0[z_it z_it'|C])>=c_z>0`

uniformly in `(i,t)` and regular common-shock realizations, then for every coefficient collection, without any
rank restriction,

`E_0[sum_it Y_it(Delta)^2|C]
 =sum_it delta_it' E_0[z_it z_it'|C] delta_it
 >=c_z sum_it ||delta_it||_2^2
 =c_z ||Delta||_F^2`.

The same conclusion follows from a lower eigenvalue bound on the full conditional population prediction Gram
operator, even when it is not represented pointwise. Such a primitive is rank-independent and would imply
identification on both `D_max` and `D_max^+` with no further argument.

Revision 9 describes conditionally nonsingular regressor moments as a simple sufficient setting, but it does
not impose the displayed global lower-eigenvalue condition. Its maintained formal condition is restricted to
the reporting class. Hence RR2.5 is not `PASS-DERIVED`.

## Minimal explicit extension

Add the following rank-selection-specific sentence to part (i) of `a:identification`:

> For construction of the spectral rank pilot, a fixed lower bound `c_+>0` also holds for every nonzero
> `Delta in D_max^+`, where only the pilot rank caps are `bar_r_M+1` and the coefficient box is unchanged.

The constant may be smaller than the reporting-class constant, but it is the same type of deterministic
uniform bound: it does not depend on `N`, `T`, the block, or the common-shock realization.

This is classification **A: a localized wording/domain extension of the existing identification
assumption**. It is mathematically stronger than Revision 9 and must not be called unchanged. It is not a new
dependence, moment, signal, or growth primitive, and it is confined to one fixed-rank pilot class. If the
author instead chooses to state global joint-regressor Gram nonsingularity, that would be a stronger primitive
formulation than the minimal domain extension needed here.

## Assumptions unaffected

The extension changes none of: dynamic stability, sequential exogeneity, spatial geometry, mixing/NED,
moments, strong low-rank signal, incoherence, coefficient bound or margin, loading/factor Gram conditions,
target regularity, or rectangular growth. Supplied-rank estimation and inference continue to use the original
`R_max` domain; only the spectral-pilot corollary invokes the enlarged clause.
