# Rectangular-rate check

Enlarging every fixed pilot cap by one changes only fixed constants in nuclear-norm inequalities and covering
entropy. It does not alter any stochastic scale. In particular,

`b_NT=(NT)^(1/(8+eta))log(NT)`

and

`zeta_NT=b_NT^2 log(NT)^(d_s+2)(1/N+1/T)`

are unchanged. The maintained condition remains

`(NT)^(4/(8+eta)) log(NT)^(d_s+6)(1/N+1/T)->0`.

The score matrix bound still uses the rectangular variance terms `N+T`; the quadratic proof still sets
`m=N+T`; and the final pilot rate is `sqrt(NT zeta_NT)`. No proof step replaces `1/N+1/T` by one component.

Accordingly, `N` and `T` may continue to diverge at any unequal rates admitted by the existing condition.
There is no new `N asymp T`, finite aspect-ratio, or polynomial-ordering requirement.
