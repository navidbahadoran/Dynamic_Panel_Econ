# Assumption and notation consequences

## Route A

Remove the pilot-only extension in Assumption 8 from the main assumptions. Remove `R_max^+`, `D_max^+`, pilot caps, the cap+1 objective-gap condition, normalized pilot spectra, ridge ratios, and the zero-rank anchor from the paper's operative notation. Restore the original supplied-rank identification domain and retain all assumptions needed by supplied-rank recovery, Riesz construction, split correction, spatial variance, and feasible normality.

If no ridge-ratio theorem remains in the submitted paper, the enlarged identification clause should not survive in an appendix. Unused assumptions and notation make the main contribution look more restrictive than it is.

## Route A+

Remove pilot-only notation from the main text and localize it to the auxiliary appendix theorem. Assumption 8's `D_max^+` clause, `R_max^+`, the approximate-global-objective condition, and ridge objects remain only in that appendix. The main supplied-rank theorem statements must not cross-reference them.

## Route B

The mathematical stochastic assumptions might remain unchanged, but the numerical approximation premise and diagnostic interpretation would change. A new link between revised acceptance and the theorem's objective-gap requirement would be needed before the assumption map could be frozen.

## Route C

`D_max^+` may disappear if the replacement pilot is not a cap+1 constrained fit, but estimator-specific rate, penalty, residual-replacement, or spectral-separation assumptions would replace it. Their exact form cannot be stated until the pilot and theorem are designed.

Minimal notation strongly favors Route A.
